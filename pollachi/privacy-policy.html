Prayaan Cabs Privacy Policy

We collect only the information required to respond to enquiries, process ride bookings, coordinate drivers and improve customer support.

Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, pickup/drop details and travel preferences submitted through forms or WhatsApp.
  • Trip details required for booking coordination and support.
  • Basic analytics information to improve website performance and marketing.

How We Use It

Information is used for booking support, trip coordination, customer communication, service improvement and legally required records.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Booking details may be shared with assigned drivers or service partners only when required to complete the requested service.
